# NALEX — Car Rental & Tours Website

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a modern, premium, responsive website for **NALEX**, a company operating in two closely connected areas:

1. **Car Rental**
2. **Tours & Travel / Driver-Guided Experiences**

NALEX should not be presented as only a traditional car rental company. The website must communicate that NALEX provides both transportation and travel experiences.

The core idea is:

> **NALEX helps people travel, explore and discover destinations comfortably, safely and reliably.**

The website should position NALEX as a professional transportation and travel partner for:

- Individual travelers
- Tourists
- Families
- Business travelers
- Hotels
- Companies
- Organizations
- International visitors
- People looking for reliable vehicles with professional driver-guides

The website should feel trustworthy, premium, adventurous, professional and welcoming.

# 15. TOUR BUSINESS LOGIC

The website should support the following conceptual flow:

### Step 1

Customer discovers NALEX.

### Step 2

Customer chooses:

**Car Rental**

or

**Tours & Travel**

### Step 3 — Car Rental

Customer selects:

- Vehicle
- Rental dates
- Pickup location
- Drop-off location
- Driver option, if applicable

### Step 3 — Tours

Customer selects:

- Destination
- Preferred date
- Number of travelers
- Tour duration
- Vehicle preference
- Driver-guide preference
- Special requests

### Step 4

Customer submits a booking/request form.

### Step 5

NALEX receives the request.

### Step 6

NALEX contacts the customer to confirm:

- Availability
- Price
- Schedule
- Pickup details
- Driver-guide
- Additional requirements

### Step 7

Booking becomes confirmed after NALEX confirmation.

IMPORTANT:

Unless a real online payment/booking backend exists, do NOT pretend that the website has real-time booking availability or automatic payment.

The website should initially function as a professional **booking/request platform**.
