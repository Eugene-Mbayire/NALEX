About NALEX

Your journey, our priority.

NALEX is a car rental and tours company built around one simple idea: your journey should be safe, comfortable, reliable and memorable.

We provide vehicle rental and travel solutions designed for different types of travellers — from people who simply need a dependable vehicle, to visitors looking for a more personalised way to discover destinations like Akagera National Park.

Our driver-guide service adds a human touch to transportation. Instead of simply taking customers from one point to another, our professional driver-guides accompany travellers, help them navigate destinations, and contribute to a more comfortable and enjoyable travel experience.

Whether you’re travelling for business, leisure, tourism or personal needs, NALEX aims to provide dependable transportation and excellent customer service from the beginning of your journey to the end.
